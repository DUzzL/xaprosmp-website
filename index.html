who we are

A chill, cozy server

xaprosmp runs on Fabric with some vanilla tweaks for quality of life. We've got a regulated shop district at spawn, a community Nether hub, and players who actually finish their builds. Mostly.

True Vanilla

We chose Fabric over Paper or Spigot to keep the gameplay Vanilla while improving performance. No game-changing mods, just QoL stuff like AFK display, mini blocks, and multiplayer sleep.

Good and fair moderation

Griefing, stealing, X-ray and seed-mining all get people banned. We're a safe place. Discrimination and harassment aren't tolerated!

Build what you want

There are very little restrictions on what you can do. You can build an industrial hellscape or a beautiful kingdom!

Help us grow 💜

To achieve xapros world domination, we need your daily votes. We don't give rewards for voting, but we'd appreciate the help.
