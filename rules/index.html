OUR RULES

Welcome to xapros SMP!

Of course we have to follow some rules so everyone has a good time on our server. They aren't restrictive, you probably won't find surprises here. Please still take your time to read them before applying to the server.

01 Social rules

  • Discrimination, harassment and similar are not tolerated here. Respect your fellow players. We are a safe place, people trying to destroy that will be banned.
  • While we allow casual swearing, we do not permit the discussion of sexual or drug related topics. We also ask you to keep political discussions to a minimum.
  • These rules also apply in private chats.
  • Follow the Discord TOS.

02 General Minecraft rules

  • We don't allow griefing (destroying others' builds), cheating (mods or hacked clients to gain an advantage) and stealing. Don't use other people's farms without their permission.
  • We explicitly highlight that X-RAY (making non transparent block transparent) will get you banned. Using the seed, lava transperancy etc to find ores is also considered X-Ray, you will still be banned.
  • You are allowed to dupe TNT, sand/concrete/gravel, carpets and rails. This is used for many farms, that's why we allow it.
  • The focus of our server is not PVP, but we don't ban it. Only fight other people after explicit consent. When someone dies, collect their items for them.
  • See the list of allowed and disallowed mods → If you have a question, don't hesitate to ask.
  • Please do your part in helping server performance. We don't have any limits on the farms you can build, but we do ask you limit the amount of animals and unnecessary redstone. Only use chunkloaders when absolutely necessary, they are not allowed to just keep farms loaded.
  • Lazy chunk TNT contraptions like some mining machines are not allowed.
  • We do not allow luck based gambling. Skill based machines like shooting ranges are allowed.
  • Using the seed to find structures including copper/iron ore veins is allowed.

These rules are subject to change. Please check them from time to time.

03 Spawn / Shopping District rules

  • Spawn is a communal space, it cannot be used for personal bases or storages. Major changes need to be approved by staff.
  • Keep entities and redstone to a minimum. Always remember that not everyone has the best hardware. We are doing this to help their FPS.
  • If you decide to build a shop, look for a free unclaimed spot. Make sure to leave some space to your neighbor. In doubt, talk to them and/or staff.
  • Shops can be removed after 21 days of inactivity. To extend that, open a ticket and let us know.
  • The builds have to be fitting. Low effort or oversized builds are not allowed and might be removed. In doubt open a ticket.
  • Shops need to have a sign listing the shops name, the owners name and the date of creation. That makes it easier to identify them.
  • You are not allowed to undercut other shops' prices. We don't want the shopping district to ruin the server economy.
  • To find items in the shopping district, use the search engine on our website → To submit your own shop, open a ticket and list the shop's name, location, owners, items sold and prices.
  • While general shops are allowed, specialized shops will be listed first in the search engine. They also have the right to offer a big amount of items at a discount of 25%.
  • You cannot sell gunpowder or TNT directly in shops. In person sales are allowed.

04 Nether & End rules

  • You are encouraged to build Nether Highways to connect your bases. They are a great way to travel to other parts of the world.
  • You are allowed to use the Nether roof for building farms, but not for normal builds.
  • Please spawnproof everything so Ghasts don't break farms. Put halfslabs on portals.
  • The main End island is a communal space. Do not use it for mining.
  • You can respawn the Dragon. It won't be able to destroy anything.
  • The End outside a radius of 3000 gets regenerated from time to time. Don't build there.
  • Do not use the connected Inner End Gateways for farms!

05 Important!

  • If you are unsure about something, open a Discord ticket and ask us about it!
  • Trying to get around the rules is not going to save you!
  • Second accounts can be addeed after 5000 minutes of active playtime.

06 Datapacks & Mods

Our server runs on Fabric. That's a modloader for servers that keeps the game vanilla, unlike Paper or Spigot. We chose to improve vanilla gameplay and performance a bit, with the following datapacks and mods.

Vanilla Tweaks

  • AFK DisplayTurns your name grey after 5 minutes of inactivity
  • Anti Enderman GriefEndermen can't pick up blocks
  • Anti Ender Dragon GriefEnder dragons won't destroy blocks
  • Customizable Armor StatuesThey look very cool
  • Mini BlocksGet mini versions of blocks
  • More Mob HeadsAll mobs have a chance to drop heads
  • Player Head DropsDrops a head when killed by another player
  • Silence MobsNametag a mob "silence_me" to silence them
  • Villager Workstation HighlightShows you a villager's workstation
  • Invisible Item Frames/trigger if_invisible while looking at one
  • Multiplayer SleepSkips the night when one player sleeps
  • Phantom ToggleNo clientside installation neccessary! Do "/phantomspawning true/false" to toggle phantoms.

Server-supported Mods

  • Simple Voice ChatOptional mod to talk to other people ingame
  • Armor-PoserClient-side alternative to armor-statues, much more efficient
  • ServuxLets players use MiniHUD features
  • FsitSit and crawl with a command

Fire Tick is off. Fires won't spread or go out without a player putting them out.
